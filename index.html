FAQ

Why should I come to QDay? +

Because the conversation happening in that room won't happen anywhere else. QDay is not a conference. It's a focused conversation on the quantum threat to cryptography, in one room, for four hours. No panels for optics. No keynotes for clout. If you're building, funding, or governing systems that rely on public-key cryptography, this is where you need to be.

What is QDay and who organises it? +

QDay is a half-day invite-only summit on the quantum threat to cryptography, organised by Quantus. The event brings together researchers, protocol engineers, institutional investors, and policy representatives to address the post-quantum migration challenge.

Is QDay open to the public? +

No. QDay is invite-only and attendance is curated. All registrations are reviewed by the organising team. If you work in quantum computing, cryptography, protocol development, institutional capital, or policy, register on Luma and we'll be in touch.
